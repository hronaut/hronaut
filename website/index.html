Why Hronaut

A real browser session, not a disposable automation process.

01

Visible by design

Watch every tab and navigation. Take over manually whenever login, consent, or visual judgment needs a person.

02

Persistent profiles

The shared Default profile survives indefinitely. Isolated workspace profiles survive restarts and archives, then disappear when their workspace is permanently closed.

03

Agent independent

Connect Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code/Copilot, or another Streamable HTTP MCP client.

04

Does not disappear

Closing the window hides it to the tray. Disconnecting an AI client leaves the browser and MCP server alive.

05

Typed and tested

The application is TypeScript-first, with Vitest unit coverage and Playwright tests against the real Electron process.

06

Your preferred look

Follow your system appearance automatically, or keep Light, Dark, or Cyberpunk fixed for this profile.

07

Updates on your terms

Check automatically, then choose when to download, install, and restart from an in-app prompt.

08

Secure by default

Each profile gets an owner-only MCP token. Authentication can be changed immediately in Settings when a trusted local workflow needs it.

09

Pause agents instantly

Reject new MCP commands with one click while every tab, login, and browser session stays exactly where you left it.

10

See the work add up

Review local, content-free action history, timing, outcomes, and success rate on Hronaut Home without recording page data.

11

Passwords stay protected

Save logins with explicit consent, OS-backed encryption, metadata-only management, and autofill that pauses agents before decrypting.

12

Use familiar shortcuts

Focus the address bar, open, close, reopen, search, and cycle tabs with the standard keyboard shortcuts you already know.

13

Choose the local port

Move the live MCP listener from Settings with validation and automatic rollback if the requested port is unavailable.

14

Zoom the page, not the chrome

Scale any site from 50% to 300% with familiar shortcuts, visible controls, or the MCP inspection tool.

15

Control where every file goes

Choose a download folder or ask where to save each file, then track progress, cancel transfers, and reveal completed downloads.

16

17

Keep useful pages close

Save local bookmarks with a familiar shortcut, then search, rename, remove, or open them yourself or through MCP.

18

Find where you were

Search a bounded 90-day history of local web visits, reopen a page, remove one entry, or clear everything yourself or through MCP.

19

Find any open tab

Search every open website by title or address, switch with the keyboard, or close clutter even after the tab strip overflows.

20

Global browser controls remain aligned in one top strip on Home and websites, while the address row contains only page-specific actions.

21

Drag tabs into your preferred order, keep important sites compact and left-most, and restore the same arrangement after restarting.

Find local pages in one place

Type once in the address bar to switch open tabs or reopen matching bookmarks and history, with private on-device suggestions.

23

Clear only what you intend

Review local history, cookies, and cache, then remove selected categories without touching bookmarks, passwords, settings, or open tabs.

24

Tame tab clutter safely

Search open and recently closed pages together, restore any chosen tab, close duplicates or tabs to the right, and keep pinned tabs protected.

25

Choose where searches go

Send submitted address-bar and agent searches to Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave Search, or Startpage while local suggestions stay private.

26

Spend less context on screenshots

Let agents capture lossless PNG or compact JPEG, constrain either dimension without distortion, and inspect hidden tray tabs just as reliably.

Never get stuck behind a dialog

Accept or dismiss open alerts and confirmations, or answer those and text prompts as part of the action that opened them.

28

Record JavaScript and CSS coverage while loading and exercising a page, then rank sanitized resources by unused bytes without exposing their source to an agent.

52

Trace an error to its source
